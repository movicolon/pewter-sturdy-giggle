# RAZOR

*Two philosophers walk into a central bank.*

*Diogenes says: throw out everything you don't need.*
*Occam says: one cause is enough.*

*Applied here: the Federal Reserve Act, cut to the bone.*

## Cut 2 — One paragraph

The United States is divided into twelve banking districts, each with a Reserve Bank. Those banks are supervised by a seven-member Board of Governors appointed by the President. Together, the Board and its Open Market Committee control how much money exists — by setting reserve requirements, printing Federal Reserve Notes, and buying or selling government bonds. Member banks must hold reserves, submit to examination, and deal with affiliates at arm's length. In exchange, the Fed lends to them in a crisis. Earnings go to the Treasury. Congress may change the rules whenever it wants.

---

## Cut 3 — One sentence

A public body controls the money supply, lends to banks in trouble, and answers to Congress.

---

## Cut 4 — Three words

**Lend. Stabilize. Oversee.**

---

*What remains after both razors: a lender that steadies the system and reports to elected government.*
*Everything else in the Act is detail about how.*
